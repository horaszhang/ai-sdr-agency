# 冷邮件模板库

## 模板 A - 基于公司新闻

**适用**: 刚获得融资、扩张、发布新产品

```
主题: Congrats on {News} - quick question

Hi {FirstName},

Saw that {Company} just {News}. That's huge - congrats!

With the growth you're seeing, I imagine outbound is becoming a bigger priority.

We help Series {A/B/C} {Industry} companies automate their cold outreach while keeping it hyper-personalized. One client in {SimilarSpace} is booking 15-20 meetings/month with zero manual work.

Worth a brief call to explore if it fits {Company}'s current needs?

Best,
Horace
```

---

## 模板 B - 基于痛点

**适用**: 销售团队较小, 需要快速扩张

```
主题: {Company}'s outbound challenge

Hi {FirstName},

Most {Industry} companies I talk to have the same challenge:
- Need more pipeline
- Don't have time for manual prospecting
- Generic cold emails don't work anymore

We solved this with AI-powered SDR that:
✓ Researches each prospect individually
✓ Writes personalized emails at scale
✓ Books meetings automatically

Result: 15-20 qualified meetings/month

Open to a 15-min call to see how this could work for {Company}?

Horace
```

---

## 模板 C - 直接价值

**适用**: 决策快、结果导向的 CEO

```
主题: 20 meetings/month for {Company}

Hi {FirstName},

I help {Industry} CEOs book 15-20 qualified sales meetings per month without lifting a finger.

How:
→ AI researches your ideal prospects
→ Writes personalized cold emails
→ Follows up automatically
→ Books meetings on your calendar

One client: 60 emails/day, 18 meetings/month, $0 in ads.

Interested in seeing if this works for {Company}?

Horace
AI SDR Agency
P.S. We only work with 5 companies at a time. {SlotStatus}
```

---

## 模板 D - 简短版

**适用**: 高管、时间紧张

```
主题: Quick question about {Company}

Hi {FirstName},

Do you handle outbound at {Company}?

We automate personalized cold email at scale. 15-20 meetings/month.

Worth a 10-min chat?

Horace
```

---

## 模板 E - LinkedIn 连接

**适用**: LinkedIn 连接后跟进

```
主题: Following up from LinkedIn

Hi {FirstName},

Thanks for connecting!

I help {Industry} companies automate their outbound and book more meetings. Saw {Company} is growing fast - figured there might be synergy.

Happy to share what's working for similar companies if you're open to it.

Best,
Horace
```

## 个性化变量

| 变量 | 示例 | 如何获取 |
|------|------|----------|
| {FirstName} | William | Apollo / LinkedIn |
| {Company} | Statsig | Apollo / LinkedIn |
| {News} | raised $100M | Crunchbase / News |
| {Industry} | SaaS | Apollo / Website |
| {SimilarIndustry} | feature flagging | Website research |
| {SlotStatus} | 2 spots left this month | Your CRM |

---

## 主题行公式

1. **Congrats + Question**: "Congrats on Series C - quick question"
2. **Specific Result**: "20 meetings/month for {Company}"
3. **Curiosity**: "The outbound playbook that actually works"
4. **Direct**: "Outsourced SDR for {Company}"
5. **P.S.**: "P.S. Saw your post about {Topic}"

---

*使用提示: 模板是起点，每封邮件必须根据具体 prospect 个性化调整。*
